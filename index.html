Need Help?

Find answers to frequently asked questions

Who can request a Microsoft email?
All registered students and faculty/staff members of Taita Taveta University are eligible for a Microsoft 365 institutional email account.
What's the difference between student and faculty licenses?
Both licenses include the same Microsoft 365 features, but they're assigned based on your role at the university to ensure proper access control.
How long does the process take?
The entire self-service process takes less than 5 minutes. Account creation is instant once you complete all steps.
What if I forget my password?
You can reset your password through the Microsoft password reset portal. Contact IT support if you need assistance.
Is my data protected?
Yes, TTU follows the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019. Your data is encrypted and processed securely.
